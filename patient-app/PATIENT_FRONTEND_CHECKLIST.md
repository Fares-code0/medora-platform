### Backend dependency

`—` means no backend is required. `BE-nn` refers to audit §15.3. `AD-n` refers to an architecture decision in audit §15.4 that must be made by the project owner before the row can start.

`API-Pnn` refers to the multi-organization API dependencies below (added 2026-10-06). The contracts are this app's **proposal** for the Backend team to confirm or change; none is implemented. Every organization-scoped route takes the organization from the session or the URL's public id, never widens access from a client parameter, and answers 404 for another patient's or organization's private resource.
